About

Two arenas.
One discipline.

Syed Hamza Ahmed Saeed Arain is a Pakistani athlete and captain of the Pakistan National Soft Tennis Team, who also competes in Asian Mind Sports. Two worlds that ask for very different things: explosive footwork and fast hands on the court, calm calculation and nerve over the board.

From two gold medals at the University of Karachi to a 3rd-place finish at the Korea Cup 2026 and Pakistan’s colours at the 20th Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Hamza has played his way from Pakistan to the international stage, and he represents his country with pride wherever he competes.

Soft Tennis

Born in Japan and played across Asia, Soft Tennis uses a lighter, softer rubber ball and rewards speed, spin and court craft, in singles and doubles. It is a fixture of the Asian Games.
